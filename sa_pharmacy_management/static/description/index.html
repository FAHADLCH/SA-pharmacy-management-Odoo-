FEFO Dispensing

A true First-Expiry-First-Out removal strategy that Odoo doesn't offer natively. The batch closest to expiry is always suggested and picked first — automatically.

Drug Interaction Screening

Patient allergies are matched to active ingredients. If a medicine conflicts, PharmaCore blocks the dispense until a pharmacist resolves it. No silent mistakes.

Controlled Substance Register

Scheduled drugs are flagged automatically and every dispense is logged with pharmacist, patient and batch — an audit trail your regulator will love.
